## Conflicts and exactness decisions

1. Fixed radius does not make Coriolis force zero in every rotating frame.
   It vanishes in ground and the corotating carrier under the stated planar
   conditions; its total power is zero in every frame by the cross product.
   The radial-motion section states this distinction explicitly.
2. A held ring has zero ground mesh work but generally nonzero rotating-frame
   mesh/shaft work. Sun and ring contact sides are kept separate; a verbal
   interchange in source observations is not inherited.
3. Hooke mean counts need finite endpoint ripple. The complete phase lift
   and partial-cycle correction are retained; a whole-cycle result is not
   promoted to arbitrary finite intervals.
4. Source zero-power loci described as rays are lines unless an additional
   half-plane restriction is supplied. The article does not rely on a count
   of such rays to infer work or event completeness.
5. The article's rational simple-train inertias and rates are independently
   declared. Different source numerical controls are not compared as if their
   parameters matched. Identical shared controls (stator heat, compound ratios,
   finite reference endpoint, hidden capacitance, common preparation) agree.
6. Original ideal successes for compound gear and terminal correspondence,
   bounded event completeness, and finite release/preparation controls remain
   mathematical successes in their stated domains. Their open physical
   questions, wider state spaces, and outstanding numerical issues remain
   distinct from the article's new finite analytic examples.
